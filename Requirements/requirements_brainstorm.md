# Requirements Brainstorm

## Problem Statement

Vaccination Cohort & Dose Scheduling System

## Functions Identified

- Enforce minimum interval between vaccination doses
- Register citizen demographic information
- Manage vaccination center slots
- Book vaccination appointments
- Record administered vaccination doses
- Generate QR vaccination certificates
- Verify QR vaccination certificates

## Constraints Identified

- A subsequent dose must not be booked before the required minimum interval.
- Vaccination slots have limited capacity.
- QR certificate verification must meet the specified performance target.
- Citizen demographic and vaccination information must be protected.

## Initial Actors

- Citizen Registrant
- Vaccination Officer
- Public Health Administrator
